Common Questions
Frequently Asked
Do I need to pay in advance? +
No advance payment is required for any booking. You pay the driver directly after your ride is complete — in cash or via UPI.
Can I cancel a booking? +
Yes, you can cancel anytime before the driver departs. Call or WhatsApp us and we'll handle it immediately — no cancellation fee.
Are the prices fixed or negotiable? +
All package prices are fixed and transparent. No hidden charges. Extra km and waiting time rates are communicated upfront at the time of booking.
Do you have drivers available at 3 AM for airport pickups? +
Absolutely. We operate 24/7, 365 days a year. Our airport transfer drivers are specially trained for early morning and late night pickups.
What is covered under the outstation package? +
The per-km rate covers driver charges and fuel. Tollgate fees, state permits, and driver accommodation (for overnight trips) are charged separately as actuals.
How do I get a booking confirmation? +
After booking, a WhatsApp message is automatically sent to your phone with all trip details. Our team will also call to confirm within 15 minutes.
