Who We Are
Founded on trust,
built on class

Amana Cab's was born in 2018 with a single, clear purpose — to give the people of Hyderabad a cab experience that feels truly premium, not just functional. We started with three cars and a belief that every passenger deserves a clean ride, a courteous driver, and a fair price.

Today, we operate a fleet of 15+ vehicles and serve thousands of passengers every month — from early morning airport runs to multi-day outstation tours across South India. Our drivers are not just chauffeurs; they're trusted companions on your journey.
